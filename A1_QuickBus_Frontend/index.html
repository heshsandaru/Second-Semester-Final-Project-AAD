About Us

We, QuickBus, are Sri Lanka’s largest online bus/coach reservation platform, transforming the way millions of Sri Lankans travel and purchase seats across the country.

QuickBus is a collaboration between Sri Lanka Transport Board (SLTB) and Hands Global Holdings (HGHL). SLTB, Sri Lanka’s state-owned transport service, operates the largest and most reliable fleet of highway and regional buses in the country. HGHL is part of a group of companies including NTC, Duro Pipe Industrial Ltd, St. Joseph’s Exports (Pvt) Ltd, and Duro Capital (Pvt) Ltd.

We offer online bus ticket booking services, a 24/7 customer support hotline (1315), bus tracking, and fleet management. Our secure payment gateways are powered by People’s Bank PLC.

Customers can modify bookings based on our seat cancellation policy. You will be notified of any schedule changes, and our mobile app allows bus tracking and seat reservations up to one month in advance.
